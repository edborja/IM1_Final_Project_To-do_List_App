Data Deletion Instructions

If you have logged into this application using Facebook and wish to have your data completely removed from our system, please follow these instructions:

Option 1: Remove via Facebook

  1. Go to your Facebook Account Settings.
  2. Navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites.
  3. Find our application in the list of active apps.
  4. Click Remove. This will revoke our access to your data.

Option 2: Complete Database Deletion

If you want your entire account, all tasks, categories, and pomodoro logs permanently deleted from our servers, please contact the developer (Edmar / CCCS 105 Group) directly, or log in to the application and use the "Delete Account" feature if available.
